Miner

  • Miner is a multiplayer horror game prototype that you have to find gold in dungeons while a blind dengreos ghost is chasing you.
  • This ghost has an advanced AI system with multiple abilities and behaviors that created with Behavior Tree.
  • I created the whole part of the enemy, its AI systems, and its interactions with player and environment. also I worked on and polished many parts of the game.
  • Developed with Unreal Engine, C++, and Blueprint.

Internship Project: FPS Tower Defence

  • This is a first-person shooter tower defence game that we created in the internship program.
  • In this game, you have to defend the base by killing the enemies by creating multiple towers.
  • But towers are not enough, and you have to deal with enemies yourself with your gun.
  • I created whole parts of interactions with towers, shop and currency system, HUD, and UI/UX of the game.

Egg League

  • A two-player game where you and your friend battle it out to collect eggs.
  • The map is filled with obstacles, and your opponent will be trying to stop you. You can also attack each other, and if you're lucky, you can steal your opponent's eggs!
  • Egg League is a fun game that will test your skills and your friendship. So gather your friends and get ready for a wild ride!
  • Developed with Unreal Engine and Blueprint.
